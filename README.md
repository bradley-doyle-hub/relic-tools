# Relic tools

Small tools for Relic Piercing & Tattoo, hosted at https://relic.bradleydoyle.ca.

## Signature generator

Email signature generator for Relic Piercing & Tattoo. Pick a name, copy, paste into your mail settings.

Lives at `/signature/`. Each tool gets its own folder.

## Invoice maker

Invoice maker for Relic Piercing & Tattoo. Fill in the form, pick Print (B&W) or Digital (colour), then print or save as PDF. Nothing is stored online.

Lives at `/invoice/`.
